# Asper

> **Prize winner: Best use of AI on a real inspection** at soonHacks.
> See the Devpost write-up: [devpost.com/software/asper](https://devpost.com/software/asper)

AI runs the office; people inspect. One continuous case from a customer's first concern to an accepted quote, and a hub that
remembers every customer, follows up with what they actually asked, and checks on them when a rainfall warning hits their area,
within Canada's anti-spam and privacy rules.
